
# Combat Progression Playtest

**Development Stage:** Very Early Development  
**Focus:** Combat Progression  
**Stages Tested:** Whispering Woods, Goblin Stronghold

## Observations

- Combat felt too easy and boring, providing little incentive to keep watching or engaging with the fights.
- Death had no meaningful consequence because experience was retained and there was no apparent penalty for dying.
- Regular waves were grindable, but their primary purpose appeared to be gaining experience rather than preparing for a meaningful challenge.
- Stage progression required defeating the boss, but the boss encounter lacked the sense of player-controlled preparation I wanted.
- Bosses felt more threatening than regular wave monsters because they had skills, whereas regular waves relied more on enemy numbers.
- I liked having multiple monsters appear simultaneously in some waves.
- HP resets between waves, which limits the potential value of potions during regular wave progression.

## Player Expectations

- Regular waves should provide a useful opportunity to grind and collect resources.
- Players should be able to choose when to challenge a boss.
- Boss encounters should provide a meaningful test of character strength and preparation.
- Death should have consequences without necessarily erasing earned experience.
- Combat difficulty should give players a reason to improve their characters rather than simply watch repetitive fights.
